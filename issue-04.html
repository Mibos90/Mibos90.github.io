🥭 The Two Months That Lasted Forever

In the 90s, May and June were not just months—they were a distinct dimension. With no smartphones, schools closed, and parents resting after lunch, afternoon boredom forced creativity.

You watered the straw *khas* pads of the dessert cooler, counted passing blue-and-yellow Indian Railways coaches, and played endless carrom matches sprinkled with Boric powder on the dining table.
