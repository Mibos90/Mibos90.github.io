Weekly Feature: The Natraj Pencil & The Blank TDK Cassette

Before Spotify playlists and YouTube algorithms, music curation required patience, sharp reflexes, and a pencil. You sat next to the two-in-one tape deck with your index fingers poised over both the Play and Record buttons simultaneously, waiting for the radio jockey to stop talking so you could capture that one Bollywood track onto a 60-minute cassette.

And when the tape inevitably unspooled into a magnetic bird’s nest inside the player? You didn't throw it out. You carefully stuck the hexagonal end of an HB pencil into the sprocket and hand-cranked the ribbon back into place with surgeon-level focus.
