📻 Weekly Story: The 2-in-1 Recorder Ritual

In middle-class living rooms across India, the two-in-one stereo system sat atop a showcase shelf, covered by an embroidered lace runner so it wouldn't catch dust.

Recording your favorite song off FM or Vividh Bharati was an olympic sport. You had to time pressing [Play] and [Record] together the millisecond the radio announcer went quiet, praying no family member called your name across the room mid-song. And when the ribbon got tangled? A hexagonal Natraj pencil was your only hope.
