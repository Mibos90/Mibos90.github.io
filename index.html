✏️ The Last Page of the Rough Notebook

Before tablets, smartphones, and app stores, the greatest gaming console in the world was the back page of an unruled register notebook. Armed with only a blue ballpoint Reynolds 045 or a leaky Camlin fountain pen, we built entire multiplayer ecosystems right under the teacher's nose.

  • Raja Mantri Chor Sipahi: Four torn bits of square paper, folded tight and tossed into the air. The suspense of Mantri squinting across the wooden bench to guess who was the Chor!
  • Dots & Boxes (Houses): A grid of pen dots where one careless move handed your benchmate a cascade of 8 closed boxes labeled with their initial.
  • FLAMES: Calculating the secret romantic fate between you and your crush on the inner cover of a Hindi notebook.
  • Name, Place, Animal, Thing: The mad scramble to invent an animal starting with "Q" or "X" before someone yelled STOP!
