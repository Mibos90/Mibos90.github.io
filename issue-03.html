📖 The Annual Ritual of Brown Paper & Name Slips

Every academic year began not with textbooks, but with scissors, Fevicol, and massive rolls of brown wrapping paper spread across the living room carpet. Every single corner had to be tucked with hospital precision and taped down with cello-tape.

The crowning glory was the glossy label stuck on the bottom right: Name, Class, Section, Roll No., Subject. If you had the fancy labels with Sachin Tendulkar or He-Man on them, you were the uncrowned king of the front row.
