🎮 When 9999-in-1 Only Meant 4 Games

Long before PlayStation 5 and ray tracing, our definition of portable computing was a gray plastic brick powered by two AA batteries that made high-pitched 8-bit screams.

The box proudly promised "9,999 GAMES IN 1", but as every 90s kid discovered within 5 minutes, it was really just Tetris and Snake repeated at 2,000 different speeds. Yet, we played it until our thumbs were bruised.
